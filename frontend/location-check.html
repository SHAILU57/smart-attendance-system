Location Check

Two different checks are used and they mean different things:

🗺 GPS Campus Check

Uses your device GPS to confirm you are inside the college campus geofence. GPS cannot tell your exact building, floor or room — it only verifies the campus area.

📌 Indoor Position Check (Building / Floor / Room)

Indoor positioning identifies the exact building, floor and room using Wi-Fi/BLE identifiers or Web Bluetooth. There are three clearly labelled modes:

🌐
Real - Authorized Wi-Fi/BLE identifier
Enter the exact Beacon ID or Wi-Fi SSID observed in the room.
Manual
📡
Real - Web Bluetooth scan

⚠ DEMO ONLY - this is NOT real physical positioning. Results are illustrative

Demo mode is enabled by the admin for testing only. A real deployment must use actual beacons / Wi-Fi to determine the room.

🔒 Privacy note

Your location is checked only at the moment you verify attendance. The system does not track your location continuously, and no other student can see where you are. Your face template is stored as a mathematical embedding on the server; never as a photo.
