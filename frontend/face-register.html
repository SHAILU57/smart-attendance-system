Why "Face Template" and not a photo?

Face recognition compares a live capture against a stored template using distance between embeddings. If it were only detection, the system would just notice "a face is present" — that cannot prove who the person is. Recognition (embedding comparison) is a statistical match, not 100% accurate, which is why attendance also verifies location and timetable before marking present (full pipeline arrives in Stage 4).
